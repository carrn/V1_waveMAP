Carr et al. 2025 (monkey V1 WaveMAP) — Figures 3 & 4

WaveMAP re-run on the same 606 visually-responsive neurons using waveform shape + λ·laminar position as features (λ=0: waveform only; λ=∞: position only), repeated for several cluster counts K ∈ {3,5,7,9}. Each clustering is forced to exactly K (an exact Louvain resolution where one exists, otherwise modularity-greedy merging of Louvain communities). Figures lead with the per-cluster mean spike (unit count next to the name); clusters are classified by shape into narrow-spiking (NS, cool), tri-phasic (TP, green) and broad-spiking (BS, warm), grouped NS→TP→BS. Direction index, amplitude, width, depth and layer are experimental measurements; only cluster membership changes. Stability = mean pairwise ARI across 8 random 85%-neuron subsamples; the direction-selectivity differentiation is the cluster-size-weighted std of per-cluster mean DI.
